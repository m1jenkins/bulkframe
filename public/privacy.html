Bulkframe Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Bulkframe is a browser extension that scans pages you choose, lists detected images, and downloads files you select. It is designed to run locally in your browser profile.

Data we store

Scan results, favorites, download history, rules, analytics events, scheduled scans, and settings are saved on your device using extension storage and IndexedDB. Bulkframe does not require an account.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not operate a Bulkframe backend that receives your browsing history.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • Host access is requested only so the extension can read images on pages you scan and fetch files you download.

Downloads

Files you save normally go through the browser download manager into your Downloads folder (or a subfolder you configure). If you choose an absolute folder and allow access, files are written to that folder instead. Website copyright, licensing, and terms still apply to anything you collect.
